Reusable instruments and research software, each built for — and often reused across — more than one piece.


  • csound7-max (2026) — Csound 7 embedded as a monolithic, statically-linked Max/MSP external (C, CMake; macOS universal + Windows). Automatic audio inlet/outlet sizing derived from .csd files, with a message protocol for channel management, event triggering and hot-recompilation
  • csound7-pd (2026) — Csound 7 embedded as a monolithic, statically-linked Pure Data / plugdata external (csound7~; C, CMake; macOS universal, Linux x86_64, Windows). Bundles Csound directly into the binary, no separate installation required; ships with runnable examples (oscillators, audio I/O, MIDI synthesis, table-based playback)
  • SBAS — Steel Bar Ambisonics Synthesizer (2023) — Csound, 6th-order ambisonics. Physical model of a steel bar (5 cm × 200 cm) driven by 7 oscillators, combined with AM/FM/additive/granular synthesis matrices and multiple spatialization techniques (point-source, mirrored, diffuse, random). Used in: Female Child System — Imprisonment
  • SSM — SoundScape Manipulation (2014–2017) — Csound/SuperCollider. Progressive time-stretch, frequency-shift, spectrum inversion and pitch-shift for transforming field recordings. Used in: Il limite della simbiosi, Piano Selvatico, Dusk Chorus
  • Animal Construction (2015) — Csound/SuperCollider physical-modelling synthesis reconstructing bird and insect sounds from acoustic analysis, transformable up to unrecognisability. Used in: Piano Selvatico
  • TinSound — Tin Meta-Sonification Synth (2014–2015) — SuperCollider, First-Order Ambisonics. Sonification of tin’s physical/chemical properties (density, speed of sound, state of matter, boiling/melting point, atomic radius and number) driving a complex additive synthesis engine. Used in: Through the space of crying
  • Human Atom (2014) — SuperCollider. Ambisonic sound manipulation and spatialization controlled through ECG and Leap Motion gestural input. Used in: Beyond the Human Atom
  • Pentagranulator (2011) — Csound real-time granular tool: four simultaneous granulators re-processed by a final granulator for meta-granulation. Used in: URLO — Urbino Laptop Orchestra, La Fuga, Bestiario filologico e fantastico
  • Metatron (2014) — SuperCollider. Sound generation based on the geometric figure known as Metatron’s Cube: 13 sinusoids at the fundamental frequency, 4 at scaled frequencies and 12 derived from cycloids traced within the geometry, combined with granular, phase/ amplitude-modulation and additive synthesis. Originally written for his Music & New Technologies thesis at Conservatorio Rossini, Pesaro
  • TeverEterno Multidelay (2016) — Csound. Ambisonic/binaural environment simulation and reconstruction tool, developed as a composition aid for the Liquidi Volumi TeverEterno competition and tested in the SPACE lab, Conservatorio Rossini, Pesaro; direct precursor of RealDelayrium. Used in: Lampi sull’Eni
  • Vocal Resonator (2012) — Csound. Physical-modelling multi-resonator (delay lines and filters) simulating the cistern in which Jochanaan is imprisoned. Used in: Salomè
  • Sound of Mafia (2015) — sonification of data on assets confiscated from organized crime, region by region across Italy, built in support of the Spaghetti Opendata project
  • SoundHeart (2016) — Csound. Sonic modelling based on analysis of the human heartbeat, with a GUI to control beat tempo, beat density and heart-sound timbre. Used in: Ken è morto

Systems, tools and integration work for the Sonosfera® museum installation (Pesaro) and the Fragments of Extinction project by David Monacchi.


  • Spaziografo & Spaziogramma (2019–2020) — Processing/Csound. 24K- resolution spatial visualization of ambisonic sound sources: spherical-harmonics analysis, ambisonics decoding, space/frequency/time analysis and 360° visualization inside the Sonosfera®
  • Spherical Delay (2020) — JUCE/C++ VST plugin: delay line on spherical harmonics up to 7th-order ambisonics, for content creation in Sonosfera®
  • Ambisonics Soundscape Composition Tools (2020) — SuperCollider. Frequency and time morphing of soundscapes up to 6th-order ambisonics, built for Fragments of Extinction
  • Sonosfera® control software (2020) — Arduino / Bash. Museum-operator control and management system for the Sonosfera®, Pesaro
  • Content creation pipeline (2019–2020) — Processing, MAX/MSP, JUCE/C++, FFmpeg, GLSL, Premiere, Millumin, GIMP: spectrogram generation, image creation/resizing, video editing
  • System integration (2019–2020) — Dante, DMX devices, 24K projection (Reaper/Millumin): management of the full electroacoustic chain
  • Forced-air control (2019–2020) — browser/DMX control of internal air recirculation
  • Eco-acoustic theatre @ Naturama (2017–2018, Svendborg, Denmark) — Bash scripts / Millumin: automated show control for the Fragments of Extinction program at the natural history museum
  • Binaural playback management (2016) — OSC, Reaper, SuperCollider: ambisonics→binaural conversion and show control, presented at the IUCN World Conservation Congress 2016, Hawaii
  • Leap Motion event generator (2015, Trento) — SuperCollider: synthetic sound-event generator controlled via Leap Motion for the eco-acoustic theatre prototype hosted at MUSE

Tools and code written specifically for music-technology education — Great Eugenio, the Csound / SuperCollider tutorials and the chemistry-sonification collaboration — are detailed on the page.

  • MolochVox (2021) — pitch/frequency-shifter suite, 5 AUv3 units: pitch shift, frequency shift, harmonic/inverse-harmonic/geometric series, spectral freeze and crossfade
  • MoDelayrium (2021) — 10-plugin AUv3 suite: chorus/flanger, doppler, reverb, tremolo, delay modulator, room filter, simple delay, space
  • Dystopia (2020) — modular experimental distortion, 7 AUv3 units
  • RealDelayrium (2019) — parallel multi-delay AUv3, 6 delay lines, resonant filter, frequency modulation, air filter
  • Delayrium (2019) — parallel multi-delay (Mono / Stereo / UNUS), 6 delay lines with tempo, panning and amplitude modulation
  • Esoteric Synth (2019) — generative/additive/granular synth, 5 oscillators through two pentagonal modulation matrices
  • MonoGranulator (2018) — granular synthesis for live electronics (Standalone + AUv3)
